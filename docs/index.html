About

I am a biostatistician who thinks in pipelines -- from the first preclinical experiment to the analysis plan that lands on a regulator's desk. My most recent work at GSK took me across both preclinical research and clinical assay statistics, always with the same focus: analyses that are rigorous, traceable, and decision-ready.

Trained across epidemiology, applied statistics, and public health data science at universities in Cameroon, Belgium, and France, respectively.

Beyond core work, I enjoy making statistics more accessible -- through R Shiny tools, open-source packages, and a perhaps excessive enthusiasm for Bayesian methods.
